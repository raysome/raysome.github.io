Join Us

[***📢] There are openings for Postdoc, PhD, MPhil, RA and Visiting Scholar positions. We welcome motivated students and researchers to join our team. Scholars and students having a strong foundation in GIScience, Computer Science, Urban Science or related disciplines are encouraged to apply.

If you are interested, please send your application package (including motivation letter, detailed CV, all transcripts, and preliminary research proposal) to Dr. Cao (ruicao@hkust-gz.edu.cn ).

We will review all applications promptly but may not respond to every inquiry due to high volume. If you pass the initial screening, you will receive an interview email within one month. Otherwise, please assume you have not advanced to the next round. Thank you for your understanding.
