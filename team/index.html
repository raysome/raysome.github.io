Research Group

The RUI (Responsible Urban Intelligence) Lab develops human-centered urban intelligence for understanding, simulating, and governing complex urban systems. Drawing on advances in GeoAI, Urban Informatics, and multimodal spatiotemporal data, we create intelligent methods and systems that support evidence-based urban decision-making. Our mission is to harness geospatial technologies and AI responsibly to foster more inclusive, resilient, and sustainable urban futures for the benefit of society.

Join Us

[***📢] There are openings for Postdoc, PhD, MPhil, RA and Visiting Scholar positions. We welcome motivated students and researchers to join our team. Scholars and students having a strong foundation in GIScience (including GIS, Remote Sensing, Photogrammetry, etc.), Computer Science, Urban Science or related disciplines are encouraged to apply.

If you are interested, please send your application package (including motivation letter, detailed CV, all transcripts, and preliminary research proposal) to Dr. Cao (ruicao@hkust-gz.edu.cn ).

We will review all applications promptly but may not respond to every inquiry due to high volume. If you pass the initial screening, you will receive an interview email within one month. Otherwise, please assume you have not advanced to the next round. Thank you for your understanding.
